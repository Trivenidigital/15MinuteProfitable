7. Failed Hypotheses & Dead Ends

Things that didn't work are just as valuable as things that did. Recording them prevents re-trying the same failed approaches.

Disabling stop-loss time decay kills late-window recovery plays

FAILED

Experiment: #2 | Date: 2026-02-08

Hypothesis: Keeping SL active in the last third of the window would prevent positions from bleeding to zero at resolution.

What actually happened: 0 winners in 15 trades. SL fired 9 times, cutting positions at -15% to -54%. All 13 early exits were losses. The bot went from +$48/exit avg to -$16/exit avg.

Root cause: Late-window prices are extremely volatile on 15-min markets. A -15% dip in the last 5 minutes is often a temporary swing that reverses before resolution. The time decay design (disable SL in last third) correctly models this — it lets positions ride through noise for the potential recovery to $1.00 payout. Stopping them out converts potential asymmetric winners into guaranteed small losses.

Lesson: Don't touch SL time decay. The real bottleneck is the base SL/TP thresholds (15%/25%) being too wide for the first/middle portions where time decay isn't active. Future tuning should target base thresholds, not the decay mechanism.
