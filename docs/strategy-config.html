Maker Arbitrage

Active

Places GTC limit orders on both YES and NO sides at prices below the best ask. If both sides fill, the combined cost is less than $1.00, locking in a risk-free profit at market resolution. Zero taker fees since all orders are maker (limit).

Price Lag Exploitation

Active

Directional strategy that exploits the lag between Binance spot price movements and Polymarket odds updates. When Binance spot moves significantly (e.g. BTC drops 0.1%), Polymarket odds may take seconds to adjust. The bot buys the correct side (YES for up, NO for down) before odds catch up, then exits via take-profit, stop-loss, or time-based exit.

Pure Arbitrage (Taker)

Active

Monitors YES ask + NO ask combined price. When the combined cost drops below the target threshold ($0.94), both sides are bought simultaneously via FOK taker orders. At resolution, one side pays $1.00, guaranteeing profit minus taker and winner fees. Rarely triggers since taker fees eat most of the spread.

ParameterValueDescription
